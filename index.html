ABOUT PROFLIC TECHNOLOGIES

Engineering Precision at the Micron Scale

PROFLIC Technologies is an advanced industrial metrology and precision quality engineering firm. We bridge the gap between physical manufacturing and digital CAD data using coordinate measuring machines, high-speed 3D laser scanners, and dedicated offline programming software.

Whether verifying critical aerospace castings, automotive dies, or medical components, our engineers deliver actionable GD&T inspection reports and turnkey offline CMM routines in PC-DMIS and PolyWorks.

• On-site CMM Inspection Accurate dimensional inspection at your facility
• 3D Laser Scanning High-accuracy 3D scanning and surface data capture
• Reverse Engineering & Modelling Convert scanned data into accurate 3D CAD models
• Quality Assurance Inspection documentation and quality reporting (FAIR & PPAP Reports)
• 2D/3D Design Precision drafting and 3D CAD design solutions
• CMM Training Practical training for CMM operation and programming
• 3D Printing Rapid prototyping from digital 3D models
• CMM Programming Support & Consultancy Expert support for CMM programming and inspection workflows (PC-DMIS, PolyWorks, RDMIS)
